Research / Interactive educational prototype

Explore Panel Orientation Geometry

An educational prototype created for this site. This illustrates one geometric component of solar modeling; it does not reproduce the founder’s original study or estimate electricity production.

Relative direct-beam alignment: 100.0%

Adjust the controls to draw the curve. JavaScript is needed for the interactive chart.

Assumptions and equation

For a south-facing panel in the northern hemisphere at solar noon on an idealized equinox, solar declination is zero and the angle between the panel normal and sunlight is latitude minus tilt. The relative direct-beam projection is max(0, cos((latitude − tilt) × π / 180)). The chart samples every integer tilt from 0° to 90°.

The model ignores weather, clouds, atmospheric losses, shading, temperature, diffuse light, equipment efficiency, day length, and seasonal variation. Its peak is not an annual optimal installation angle. The latitude default of 43° is an illustrative rounded value near Albany.
