Research / Technical exploration

Quantum Key Distribution and Communication Efficiency

Problem

Security mechanisms must be considered alongside communication overhead and implementation constraints.

Research question

How do classical cryptography and quantum key distribution differ in security assumptions and communication requirements?

Method

A conceptual comparison of classical cryptographic methods and quantum key distribution, informed by encryption logic and simulation-oriented reasoning.

Tools and technologies

Cryptography concepts, technical literature, and analytical comparison. No physical quantum communication system is claimed.

Result and current status

A technical exploration described in founder materials, without a published benchmark, peer-reviewed paper, or production deployment.

Learning and limits

Security and efficiency comparisons need an explicit threat model and a clear account of the resources required.

What could come next

Document the threat model and protocol assumptions, then build a reproducible educational comparison.
