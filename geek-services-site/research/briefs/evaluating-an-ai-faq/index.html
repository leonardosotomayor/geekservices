Research / Method brief

Evaluating an AI FAQ Assistant

Research question: can a bounded assistant answer routine questions reliably enough to help a team? A fluent answer alone is insufficient evidence.

Method

Choose approved source documents. Prepare representative questions, including questions the sources cannot answer. Record the expected answer or escalation behavior before testing. Include ambiguous requests and attempts to override the assistant’s instructions.

Measurement

Record whether each answer is supported, whether citations point to the right passage, and whether out-of-scope requests reach a person. Keep a separate set of unseen questions for evaluation after changes. A spreadsheet can capture question, expected behavior, observed answer, and reviewer decision.

Limits and next step

This method has not been run as a published experiment by Geek Services. Real deployments need privacy review, monitoring, and maintenance when source material changes.
