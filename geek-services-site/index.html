Useful answers before you begin

What does Geek Services build?

Websites, inquiry systems, automation, AI tools, reporting workflows, and research-driven information products. The scope depends on the problem and available evidence.

Who is a good fit?

Albany and Capital Region businesses and organizations with a clear operational problem, a digital system to improve, or a technical question to explore.

Is the Research and Information Lab a scientific institution?

It is the research division of a founder-led startup. Published project summaries describe technical explorations; they are not claims of institutional accreditation or peer-reviewed findings.

Can I visit an office?

Geek Services currently operates online. Consultations and project delivery are remote, with Albany and the Capital Region as the primary market. There is no public office or walk-in location.

How do I begin?

Send an inquiry or request a consultation. Share the current process, the problem, and what a useful outcome would look like.
