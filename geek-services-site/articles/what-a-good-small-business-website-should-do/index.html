What a Good Small Business Website Should Actually Do

A strong website should make your business easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to contact.

For most small businesses, a site should do three things well:

  1. Explain the offer clearly
  2. Build confidence quickly
  3. Turn interest into action

If it looks nice but does not move people toward contact, it is underperforming.
