Simple Marketing Metrics Small Business Owners Should Watch

You do not need a huge dashboard. You need a few signals that tell you whether your visibility and lead flow are improving.

Marketing becomes easier to manage when you track a few useful numbers consistently.

Start with:

  • inquiries or leads
  • response time
  • landing page conversion
  • top traffic sources

The point is not reporting for its own sake.

The point is making better decisions with less guesswork.

Better reporting should make the next decision easier, not create more noise.
