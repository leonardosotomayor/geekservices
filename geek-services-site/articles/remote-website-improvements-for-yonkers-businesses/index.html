Remote Website Improvements for Yonkers Businesses

Scope a focused website and follow-up project for a Yonkers or Westchester business.

Define the remote deliverable

Choose a specific problem such as an unclear service page or a contact form that collects too little context. Share the current URL and describe who handles inquiries. A short, bounded project is easier to review remotely than an undefined promise of more leads.

Prepare the handoff

Use separate collaborator access where tools support it, rather than sending passwords. Agree on who approves copy, checks submissions, and owns the system after delivery. Clearly state the actual Westchester service area without inventing office locations.

Verify after launch

Test a real inquiry with the business owner, confirm the message arrives, and check the customer confirmation. Review the inquiry fields and follow-up routine together. Geek Services provides this work remotely from its primary Albany base.
