# Geek Services LLC Website Content Foundation

## Messaging Priority

The website should answer these questions fast:

1. What does Geek Services LLC do?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problems does it solve?
4. What should the visitor do next?

## Recommended Brand Message

Geek Services LLC helps small businesses build a stronger online presence, improve customer response, and create clearer systems for lead generation, follow-up, and digital growth.

## Content Rules

Use:

- plain language
- practical value
- confidence without hype
- business-owner-friendly explanations

Avoid:

- fake portfolio claims
- inflated agency language
- overused technology buzzwords
- enterprise jargon

## Visual Direction

Recommended direction:

- modern and sharp, but approachable
- less "luxury tech"
- more "smart local growth partner"
- strong typography and contrast
- simple section structure
- clean visual cues around growth, clarity, and better systems

## Portfolio Section Guidance

Do not use a traditional portfolio grid with invented projects.

Better alternatives for the first live version:

- problem/solution cards
- service outcome cards
- example deliverables
- "what we can build for your business" section
- future case studies section marked as upcoming when real proof exists

## Contact Capture Guidance

Minimum viable live setup:

- name
- email
- business name
- short message

Helpful additions later:

- service interest
- current website status
- biggest challenge

## Future Proof Additions

Add later when available:

- real testimonials
- real client results
- before/after snapshots
- downloadable lead magnet
- scheduling integration
