# Leonardo Sotomayor Founder Portfolio Script

## Purpose

This document defines a separate founder-focused section or page for Leonardo Sotomayor. It is distinct from the Geek Services LLC service page and is meant to help visitors understand who he is, what he builds, and why he is credible.

## Positioning

This section should present Leonardo as:

- a technical founder with a strong interest in software, AI/ML, and analytical problem-solving
- a builder who can connect technical work to practical business value
- a credible, local, grounded person rather than a generic self-promotional "personal brand"

This page should not read like a full employment history. It should focus on:

- technical ability
- founder identity
- analytical projects
- education and recognition
- selected evidence of capability

## Best Fit For The Site

Recommended structure:

- separate page linked in the main site navigation as `Founder` or `About Leonardo`

Reason:

- keeps the business homepage focused on lead generation
- gives interested visitors a place to evaluate credibility
- avoids overloading the service page with resume-style content

## Recommended Page Goal

After reading this page, a visitor should think:

- he understands technical systems
- he can build, explain, and market
- he has real academic and project discipline
- he is credible enough to trust with a small business project
