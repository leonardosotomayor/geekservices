# Geek Services LLC Master Brief

## Purpose

This document is the working source of truth for the Geek Services LLC website project. It filters older deliverables and AI-generated drafts into a practical foundation we can use to launch a real website and improve it over time.

## Primary Goal

Launch a real, professional website for Geek Services LLC that:

- clearly explains what the business does
- speaks to local small-business owners
- generates consultation inquiries
- can be updated live as the business evolves

## Business Positioning

Geek Services LLC is an AI-powered digital marketing and business automation service for small businesses that need more visibility, better customer follow-up, and simpler systems.

The strongest positioning from the older materials is:

- local-first support for small businesses, especially around Yonkers and nearby markets
- practical AI adoption, not hype
- affordable and accessible help for businesses with limited time and technical resources
- clear focus on growth, automation, and measurable outcomes

## Ideal Clients

Primary audience:

- small businesses with roughly 1-25 employees
- businesses with under $500K revenue
- owners or managers handling too much manually

Strong-fit segments from the older files:

- retail shops
- restaurants and cafes
- service businesses
- creatives and musicians
- barbershops
- clothing brands

## Problems We Help Solve

- inconsistent lead generation
- weak or outdated web presence
- manual customer service bottlenecks
- inconsistent content creation
- poor visibility into marketing performance
- low automation across lead capture and follow-up

## Core Services To Feature On The Website

These are the strongest service categories supported by the old business materials:

1. AI-Powered Lead Generation
2. AI Chatbots and Customer Support
3. AI Content Creation
4. Analytics and Performance Tracking
5. Website and Funnel Setup
6. Simple Business Automation

## Delivery Style

The business should feel:

- practical
- transparent
- local
- modern
- supportive

The website should not present Geek Services as a generic software studio, enterprise SaaS firm, or fake agency with invented case studies.

## What To Keep From Prior Materials

Keep:

- `geekservicesny.com` as the working domain direction
- the local small-business focus
- the AI-powered service model
- the first-year goals of 10-15 clients and process building
- the values: accessibility, transparency, innovation, community-focus, simplicity
- the lead-gen and consultation-driven website approach

Keep selectively from the ZIP:

- the idea of a polished, modern starter layout
- responsive structure
- strong hero section
- service cards
- contact CTA

## What To Remove Or Replace

Remove or replace:

- fake software portfolio projects
- invented work history and timeline
- fake testimonials
- generic "web app studio" positioning
- any copy that makes the business sound broader than it really is
- any design choices that feel luxurious but disconnected from the local-business audience

## Recommended Website Direction

The site should position Geek Services LLC as:

"AI-powered marketing and automation support for local businesses that want more leads, faster follow-up, and a stronger online presence."

This is stronger than a generic portfolio because it gives visitors a direct reason to hire the business.

## Recommended Initial Site Structure

1. Hero
2. Who We Help
3. Services
4. How It Works
5. Why Geek Services
6. Optional starter proof/results section
7. Lead magnet or free consultation CTA
8. Contact

## Proof Strategy

Until real client case studies exist, use:

- realistic outcomes and process language
- founder/operator credibility
- clear explanation of tools and deliverables
- local focus
- transparent workflow

Avoid presenting placeholders as if they are real clients.

## Immediate Build Priorities

1. Create launch-ready site messaging.
2. Build a starter site that can go live quickly.
3. Add a contact path that captures inquiries.
4. Deploy the site and iterate in public.

## Working Rule For Future Consultant Responses

Every new recommendation should be filtered through these questions:

1. Is it true to the actual business model?
2. Does it help small local businesses understand the offer?
3. Does it move us closer to a live, inquiry-generating website?
4. Is it specific enough to keep, or is it generic filler?
